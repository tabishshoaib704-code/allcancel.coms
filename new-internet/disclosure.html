How we make money

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version: using this site is free. Some providers or their authorized retailers pay us when a customer signs up after contacting us. That never changes the price you pay, and we are not owned by any provider.

Who we are

New Internet is an independent comparison website operated by [Company legal name]. We are not an internet service provider. We do not sell, install or support internet service ourselves, and we are not affiliated with the companies listed on this site unless we state otherwise.

How we are paid

When you call us and go on to order service, the provider or the authorized retailer handling your order may pay us a referral or commission fee. The amount varies by provider and plan. You pay the same price you would pay going direct.

How that affects what you see

We show plans from both paying and non-paying providers. Compensation can influence which providers appear on the site and the order they are listed in when you have not chosen a sort option yourself. It does not change the speeds or prices we report, and we do not accept payment to describe a plan as better than it is.

You can sort the comparison table by price or speed at any time to see the list ordered on the merits rather than on any commercial arrangement.

Where our prices come from

Prices and speeds are gathered from provider websites and public sources and are reviewed periodically. They are starting rates for illustration, not offers. Promotions change frequently and availability depends on your exact address, so confirm the current terms with the provider before you order.

What we do not do

  • We do not charge you a fee for comparing plans or for calling us.
  • We do not sell your personal information. See the privacy policy.
  • We do not claim to list every provider or every plan available in the United States.
