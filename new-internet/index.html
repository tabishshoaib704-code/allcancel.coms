Questions people ask

Is New Internet an internet provider?

No. New Internet is an independent comparison service. We are not owned by or affiliated with the providers listed unless stated. We may be paid by providers or authorized retailers when you sign up through us, which never changes the price you pay.

Why do prices change by address?

Providers set prices and promotions by region, and not every plan is offered on every street. The prices on this page are starting rates; your exact price is confirmed when you check your address.

Does it cost anything to use this site?

No. Comparing plans and calling us is free.

What's the cheapest home internet?

Low-speed cable or 5G home plans usually have the lowest monthly price. Many providers also offer low-income discount programs; ask when you call.
